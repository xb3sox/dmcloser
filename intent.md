# DMCloser Product Intent

Status: implementation requested by the product owner; production release not approved.

Build a calm, mobile-first sales inbox that turns inbound Instagram/WhatsApp leads into customers. Preserve the existing landing page, dark/light identity, and credential-free demo.

Complete the missing product loop: sign up, configure business context, connect accounts, ingest signed messages, generate grounded replies, review/edit/send or safely automate, retain lead memory, manage usage and subscription, export/delete data, and observe failures.

Constraints: no browser secrets; workspace isolation; signed webhooks; no invented product facts; explicit automation consent; bounded spending; no blind duplicate sends. Missing credentials must produce clear setup states, never simulated live success.

Targets: connect an eligible pre-approved account in under two minutes; first suggested reply p95 under two seconds; no cross-workspace access or duplicate outbound side effects. These are release targets, not measured claims.

External gates: Meta approval, SMTP/OpenAI/Stripe credentials, production Postgres/TLS, privacy review, load test, restore/rollback rehearsal, owner release approval.

## Increment: Revenue OS

Evolve the working product, never rebuild it. Priority: revenue > conversion > retention > moat. Add a commercial system of record, explainable lead/deal intelligence, approved Business Brain, outcome learning, industry-aware recommendations, and review-first sales workflows. Retain the inbox, integration safety gates, tenancy, demo and visual language.

Financial truth must be explicit: payments are owner-confirmed records, won value is not collected cash, forecasts are dated/currency-specific estimates, and rule scores are not calibrated probabilities. Unknown BANT fields remain unknown. Learning must never silently turn customer claims into approved business facts or widen automation permissions.
