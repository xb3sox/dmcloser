# Business Plan
> Owner: Founder (BRNGT LLC, founder-reported) | Version: 0.5 (working draft) | Updated: 2026-10-20 | Approval: not granted

## Business Model
DMCloser provides subscription access to a business-owned, AI-assisted Meta conversation workspace with measurable lead and sales outcomes. Customer value is timely prioritized replies and inspectable evidence, not guaranteed extra revenue. Channels are official Meta integrations, not proprietary messaging. The company is BRNGT LLC per founder, legal identity not independently corroborated. Plans: Trial (synthetic/sandbox), Starter (safe Inbox and knowledge), Growth (AI allowance, qualification), Business (outcome reports and permitted follow-up). All sectors selectable; no separately trained model by vertical. **P1 pricing is unapproved** and must be tested, not guessed.

| Business Model Canvas | Lean implementation |
|---|---|
| Partners | Meta, compliant hosting/auth, privacy-reviewed LLM and checkout providers |
| Core activities | reliable messages, human-controlled AI, feedback, outcome evidence |
| Value offered | fewer lost inquiries and a credible conversation-to-outcome history |
| Segments | SMB owners and sales representatives across sectors in Saudi Arabia first |
| Channels | founder-led outreach, referrals, bilingual landing/demo |
| Relationships | self-serve trial enabled through founder-supported early pilots |
| Revenue | workspace subscription; capped AI usage/overages only if economical |
| Costs | data hosting, AI, Meta messages, payment, legal, SaaS operations |

## Market & Customer Research
Initial hypothesis: businesses receiving multiple recurring DMs lose opportunities to slow response and poor continuity. Ideal buyer has a real inbound lead stream, a staff member to accept lead actions and desire to measure booked or confirmed transactions. Shop/service selection allows property, vehicles, retail, personal services, B2B agencies, clinics for **administrative** use only, and Custom. A sector selector is not evidence of equal sales traction across all markets. There are **no observed customer interviews, demand, TAM/SAM/SOM or purchase commitments**. Proposed interview study: 12 owners in a cross-sector mix (sample, not completed), request specific anonymized recent inquiry examples, monthly messaging volume, pain, purchasing authority and subscription willingness. Do not use a made-up market-size number; size top-down from official Saudi industry lists and bottom-up from validated eligibility and paid conversion rates once sampled.

## Pricing & Monetization
Recommended packaging (working, not contracted): free guided preview only with synthetic data, Starter basic shared inbox, Growth AI credits and qualification, Business evidence ledger and outcome dashboard, Enterprise deferred. Final SAR/USD tier prices, provider transfer fees and possible per-seat charges require cost model and willingness-to-pay validation. AI spend must be capped by tenant; never advertise unlimited at unknown vendor exposure. Validate billing eligibility for BRNGT LLC and Saudi card acceptance before launching subscription checkout.

## Go-to-Market
**Message:** "See which DM needs attention, respond with accountable AI, and connect outcomes to real evidence." Enable any industry to register, but rotate targeted founder-led experiments one vertical at a time to reduce acquisition cost. Funnel: targeted opt-in outreach → discovery → narrated demo using fictional data → approved integration → first safe reply → first qualified lead → verified booking/sale → paid subscription. Proposed pilot: two compliant consented companies after technical/privacy gates; track lead response time, time-to-first-value, per-tenant provider cost and payment intent. No guarantee of booked meetings or increased revenue. A bilingual, mobile-friendly landing/demo is recommended, without paid advertising commitment.

## Financial Planning
**Founder-selected suggested prototype running envelope: USD 25–50/month** excluding unknown Delaware/US compliance, accountant, registered agent, processing and channel charges. This is not proof of actual costs or authorization to commit. Use managed trial plans and synthetic test traffic while proving channel integration. Required inputs: `N` paid workspaces, `P` price/tenant, `R` AI requests/tenant, `Tin,Tout` tokens/request, `Qin,Qout` vendor dollars/1M tokens, `Cmeta` total destination/category Meta charges, `H` cloud/DB cost, `Support` per-account hours/cost, `Fees` processor fees. Formulas: `MRR=N*P`; `AI_cost=N*R*(Tin*Qin+Tout*Qout)/1_000_000`; `COGS=AI_cost+Cmeta+H+Support+Fees`; `Gross_margin=(MRR-COGS)/MRR` if MRR>0; `Net_burn=monthly_cash_out-monthly_cash_in`; `Runway=cash/net_burn` when positive; `Break_even_accounts=monthly_fixed/(P-per_account_variable_cost)` if denominator positive. No historical performance or numerical forecasts available. Scenarios: downside (lower paid conversions/higher usage), pilot observed (actual later), upside (higher conversion subject to provider limits). Never display synthetic illustrations as forecasts. Monitor delayed vendor settlement and use hard cap plus warning threshold.

## Customer Success
Founder-led onboarding: choose sector or Custom, authorize Meta account, define approved FAQs/price list, rehearse safe messages in test environment, test handoff, inspect first lead, confirm initial outcome separately. Support is email/form and in-product reporting during founder working hours initially; no promised 24/7 SLA. P1 security (wrong-tenant exposure, unauthorized send) triggers immediate stop-send/containment. Retention measured by active connected accounts, resolved inquiries, owner-perceived value, completed outcomes, churn reason, and recoverable data export. Cancellation needs token revocation and legally appropriate data deletion.

## Legal & Compliance
**Legal determinations require qualified professionals.** Delaware LLC status, EIN, registered agent, tax and formation/ownership proof remain uninspected; Delaware LLC annual tax reference: [Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/). Saudi PDPL obligations may apply to personal data relating to Saudi residents processed abroad, including transparency, minimization and transfer considerations; counsel must determine entity role/controller vs processor, lawful bases, cross-border safeguards and any commercial establishment/tax duties. [SDAIA PDPL](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/Research/Pages/DataProtection.aspx). Draft October 2026 amendments must not be treated as enacted until official status is verified. Sign real SaaS terms, privacy notice, DPA/subprocessor terms where necessary; scope IP assignment, permissible sector services, opt-out/direct marketing, tax, refund and recurring payments. Meta permission/marketing template approvals are external conditions. Source-available licenses (particularly SaaS restrictions) require assessment before embedding any code.

## Operating Model
Founder accountable for product, engineering release, monitoring, support and vendor cost control; AI assistant can draft/review but must not authorize payments/releases/legal conclusions. Contract reviewed counsel/privacy specialist and qualified accountant when needed. Daily operational scan: errors, provider delivery statuses, queued sends, secrets alerts and spend. Weekly: user trials, prompt evaluation, security regression; monthly: costs, refunds, access grants and backup restore sample. Failures: manually service customers, suspend AI, have provider exit/export plan. Retire account by export, revoke connected resources and delete records per approved retention holds.

## Business Risks
Market willingness to pay (high); Meta permission dependency (high); legal data/cross-border scope (critical before real data); AI hallucinated offers (critical before auto-send); business evidence falsification (high); cost overrun (high); founder unavailability (medium). Countermeasures: pre-sell interviews, sandbox tests, counsel gates, mandatory approval, evidence-backed revenue ledger, spending caps, monitoring and modest SLA claims.
